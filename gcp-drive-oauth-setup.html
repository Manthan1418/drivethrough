🧠 What are “credentials”? 30 seconds: Google gives your app two strings — a name tag (Client ID, not secret) and a password (Client secret — keep it safe!). You create them once at Google; DriveThrough stores them encrypted in its Settings. Every connect then happens on Google's own page, where you pick the account and approve. No app ever sees your Google password.

💾 Tip: open a blank Notepad/Notes file now — Google shows the secret only once.
